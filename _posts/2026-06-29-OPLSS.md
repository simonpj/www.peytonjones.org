# Abstract
<!-- this H1 (denoted by the single octothorpe before the word 'Abstract') should remain unchanged. -->
  <!-- Note: Make sure to enter at least twice to create seperate lines for the page. -->

This is a series of four tutorial-style lectures, given at the Oregon
Programming Languages Summer School 2026.
The cover the programmer's-eye view of Verse, its semantics (both operational
and denotational), and how to verify (i.e. typecheck) Verse programs.

These lectures describe work in progress!
